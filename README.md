## Optimum Billing Safety Reminder

To avoid billing scams, always use trusted billing methods and official Optimum payment channels.

Optimum customers should avoid making payments through:

* Gift cards
* Cryptocurrency
* Wire transfer
* Venmo
* Zelle
* Cash App
* PayPal or other unofficial payment requests

Always confirm your balance and payment status through your Optimum account, official Optimum website, or the number printed on your bill.
